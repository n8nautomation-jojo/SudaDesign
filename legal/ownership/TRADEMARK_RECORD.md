## 4. Distinctiveness Assessment & Third-Party Disclaimers

### 4.1. Distinctiveness Analysis
- The composite mark **"Suda Design"** combines a distinctive prefix ("Suda") with a descriptive industry term ("Design").
- In trademark jurisprudence, composite marks of this structure are generally categorized as suggestive or arbitrary, but clearance against existing registrations in Class 9 is required to confirm absence of conflicting prior marks.

### 4.2. Disclaimers of Affiliation
- **Adobe Inc. (Photoshop):** Suda Design has **no affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement, or commercial connection with Adobe Inc.** Any reference to Photoshop, PSD file compatibility, or layer concepts is strictly descriptive and functional (nominative fair use).
- **Upstream PhotoCraft:** While incorporating open-source software from the PhotoCraft project under Apache-2.0 / MIT, Suda Design operates under its own distinct commercial brand identity.

## 5. Brand Usage Guidelines (Common Law Protection)

1. **Symbol Usage:** Use the `™` symbol (e.g., Suda Design™) in prominent commercial headings and store descriptions to signal common-law trademark claims until formal federal/national registration certificates are issued. (Do not use `®` prior to formal registration).
2. **Third-Party Open Source Notice:** Third parties building upon the open-source codebase must rebrand their distributions and remove "Suda Design" and the "Sd" splash logo in accordance with Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0.
