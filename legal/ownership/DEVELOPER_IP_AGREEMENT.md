### 1. ENGAGEMENT AND PURPOSE
The Company engages Developer as an independent contractor to design, develop, enhance, test, and maintain software code, modules, algorithms, UI components, documentation, and related digital materials for the project commercially known as **Suda Design** (the "Project").

### 2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT (WORK-MADE-FOR-HIRE)

#### 2.1. Work Made for Hire
Developer agrees that all software code, architecture, algorithms, user interfaces, scripts, documentation, and other works of authorship created or contributed by Developer in connection with the Project (collectively, the "**Work Product**") shall constitute a "work made for hire" for Company under applicable copyright law.

#### 2.2. Assignment of All Intellectual Property
To the extent that any Work Product does not legally qualify as a work made for hire, Developer hereby irrevocably assigns and conveys to Company, exclusively and throughout the world, all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including:
- Worldwide copyrights, patents, utility models, and trade secrets.
- All economic rights to reproduce, modify, adapt, distribute, publicly display, sublicense, and commercialize the Work Product.
- All rights to register, prosecute, and enforce copyrights, patents, and trademarks arising from the Work Product, including rights to sue for past or future infringement.

#### 2.3. Moral Rights
Developer waives, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, all moral rights (droit moral) in the Work Product. Where waiver is legally impermissible, Developer covenants not to assert such moral rights against Company, its licensees, successors, or assignees.

---

### 3. DEVELOPER REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Developer represents and warrants that:
1. **Originality:** All Work Product delivered is Developer’s sole original creation.
2. **Strict Prohibition on Unapproved Copyleft:** Developer shall **NOT** introduce, link, or combine with the Work Product any software code subject to a reciprocal copyleft license (including GPL, AGPL, or LGPL), unless explicitly authorized in advance in writing by Company.
3. **Disclosure of Permissive Components:** Developer must disclose all third-party open-source libraries proposed to be incorporated, ensuring compliance with Company’s permissive licensing requirements.
4. **No Infringement:** The Work Product does not infringe upon the intellectual property or proprietary rights of any third party or former employer.

---

### 4. CONFIDENTIALITY

Developer covenants to maintain strict confidentiality regarding all non-public source code, algorithms, unreleased features, and business plans, using a degree of care no less than reasonable care.
