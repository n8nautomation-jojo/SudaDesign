## 5. Phase 5: FinCEN Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) Reporting

- [ ] **Review Corporate Transparency Act (CTA) Rules:**
  - **Current Rule for 2025+ Creations:** Reporting companies created or registered on or after January 1, 2025 have **30 calendar days** from the date of receiving actual or public notice of creation to file their initial BOI report.
  - **Litigation Context:** Note that while several federal court decisions (such as *NSBA v. Yellen*) have challenged the constitutionality of the CTA, FinCEN currently takes the position that reporting requirements remain active for entities not specifically covered by individual injunctions.
- [ ] **File Initial BOI Report:** Submit online via the official [FinCEN BOI E-Filing System](https://boiefiling.fincen.gov/) (zero filing fee). Disclose legal name, date of birth, residential address, and passport image of `INDIVIDUAL_OWNER`.

## 7. Phase 7: Ongoing Annual Maintenance & Statutory Fees

- [ ] **Wyoming Annual Report & License Tax:**
  - **Due Date:** First day of the anniversary month of formation each year (W.S. 17-29-209).
  - **Statutory Fee:** **Minimum $60.00** or two-tenths of one mill on the dollar ($0.0002) of all assets located and employed in Wyoming, whichever is greater. Verified per official Wyoming statutes.
