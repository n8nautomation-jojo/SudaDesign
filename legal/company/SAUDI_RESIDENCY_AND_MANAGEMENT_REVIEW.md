## 2. Confirmed Legal Principles Under Saudi Law

### 2.1. Personal Income Taxation
- **Natural Persons:** Under current Saudi tax statutes administered by ZATCA, natural persons residing in the Kingdom are **not subject to personal income tax** on personal employment earnings, freelance activities, or foreign investment dividends.
- **Expatriate Fees:** Standard expatriate residence permits (Iqama) are subject to statutory dependent and government processing fees, but not progressive personal income taxation.

### 2.2. Corporate Income Tax Framework
- **Statutory Rate:** Under Article 7 of the Saudi Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. M/1 of 1425H), foreign entities operating in the Kingdom or earning taxable income from a Saudi source are subject to a **20% Corporate Income Tax** on adjusted net profits.
- **Scope:** Non-Saudi natural persons and foreign corporate entities are governed by income tax regulations, whereas Saudi and GCC nationals (and entities wholly owned by them) are generally subject to Zakat (2.5%).

## 3. Risk Scenarios Requiring Saudi Legal & Tax Advice

### 3.1. Place of Effective Management (POEM) & Corporate Tax Residency Risk
- **Statutory Rule:** Under Article 3(b) of the Saudi Income Tax Law, a capital company established under foreign laws is considered **resident in the Kingdom for tax purposes** if its **"place of central management"** is situated within Saudi Arabia.
- **Risk Scenario:** If `INDIVIDUAL_OWNER` is the sole Managing Member of `FUTURE_WYOMING_LLC` and makes all executive, architectural, commercial, and financial decisions while sitting in Saudi Arabia, ZATCA could potentially assert that the LLC has its central management located within the Kingdom.
- **Critical Caveat on Revenue:** While Suda Design currently operates free of charge with zero revenue, **zero revenue does NOT automatically eliminate corporate tax registration or reporting obligations.** Under ZATCA regulations, tax-resident entities may still be required to register, maintain audited financial statements, and file annual declarations regardless of profitability.

### 3.2. Permanent Establishment (PE) Risk
- **Statutory Rule:** Under Article 4 of the Saudi Income Tax Law, a Permanent Establishment exists if a foreign enterprise carries out business in the Kingdom through a fixed place of business or dependent agent.
- **Risk Scenario:** Conducting regular software maintenance, deployment, and customer management from a fixed residential base in the Kingdom could be evaluated under PE doctrine.
- **Action Required:** Consult Saudi tax counsel to evaluate whether management protocols, board resolutions, and US operational substance can mitigate PE characterization.

### 3.3. Commercial Activity & Anti-Concealment Regulations (*Tasattur*)
- **Statutory Restriction:** Under the Saudi Anti-Concealment Law (Royal Decree No. M/4 of 1442H) and Ministry of Commerce regulations, foreign residents on standard employment sponsorship (regular Iqama) are strictly prohibited from engaging in commercial trade or business activities for their own account without proper statutory licensing.
- **Authorized Pathways:**
  1. **Saudi Premium Residency (Investor / Entrepreneur Track):** Governed by the Premium Residency Center; provides statutory rights to own and conduct commercial activities in the Kingdom without requiring a local Saudi sponsor.
  2. **Ministry of Investment (MISA) Entrepreneur License:** Authorizes foreign entrepreneurs to establish 100% foreign-owned commercial entities in the Kingdom under structured investment criteria.
  3. **Holding Equity in Foreign Entities:** Holding passive equity in a foreign company from abroad is permissible, provided the individual does not conduct unauthorized local commercial operations within Saudi territory.

## 4. Intellectual Property Governance in Saudi Arabia (SAIP)

1. **Principle of Territoriality:** A trademark registration in the United States (USPTO) provides zero legal protection within Saudi Arabia.
2. **SAIP Protection:** The Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP) governs trademark, copyright, and patent registrations under the GCC Unified Trademark Law.
3. **Action Item:** Upon establishing legal residency, evaluate filing for trademark protection for "Suda Design" and the "Sd" logo with SAIP in Nice Class 9 and Class 42 to secure local brand exclusivity.
