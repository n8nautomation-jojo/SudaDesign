## 1. Federal Entity Classification (Disregarded Entity)

1. **Default Pass-Through Classification:** Under US Treasury Regulation § 301.7701-3 (the entity classification "check-the-box" regulations), an LLC with a single individual member is classified by default as an **"entity disregarded as separate from its owner"** (Disregarded Entity) for US federal income tax purposes.
2. **Corporate Fiction for Information Reporting Only:** Under Treasury Regulation § 1.6038A-1(c)(1) and § 301.7701-2(c)(2)(vi), a domestic disregarded entity that is 25% or more foreign-owned is treated as a domestic corporation **solely for the purposes of mandatory information reporting under Internal Revenue Code (IRC) Section 6038A**.

## 2. IRC Section 6038A Reporting: Form 5472 & Pro Forma Form 1120

### 2.1. The Legal Trigger: Reportable Transactions
Under Treasury Regulation § 1.6038A-2(a)(1) and (a)(2), a reporting corporation (or foreign-owned disregarded entity) is required to file **IRS Form 5472** with respect to each related foreign party with which it engaged in **reportable transactions** during the taxable year.

**Reportable Transactions Include:**
- Initial capital contributions (money deposited to fund the LLC or pay incorporation costs).
- Transfer or assignment of intellectual property (e.g., assigning Suda Design code or trademarks).
- Loans, advances, or repayments between the LLC and the owner.
- Payment of LLC expenses (such as registered agent fees or state filing fees) by the foreign owner from personal funds.
- Distributions, management fees, or service fees paid between the LLC and related parties.

### 2.2. Distinguishing Cases Where No Filing Obligation Arises
- **Zero-Transaction Scenarios:** If in a subsequent tax year a foreign-owned disregarded entity is completely dormant and engages in **literally zero reportable transactions** with related parties (no capital in, no money out, no transactions or transfers), Treas. Reg. § 1.6038A-2 does not on its face require a Form 5472 for that year.
- **Practical Caution:** Because ongoing maintenance fees, domain renewals, or registered agent invoices paid by the founder typically constitute related-party transactions or capital contributions, reportable transactions are present in practice in nearly every active operating year.
- **Mandate:** The owner must **NOT** assume automatic exemption, nor assume that filing is mandatory in the total absence of transactions. Each tax year’s ledger must be formally reviewed by a US international tax CPA.

### 2.3. Statutory Penalty for Non-Filing: $25,000 USD
Under IRC Section 6038A(d) and Treas. Reg. § 1.6038A-4(a), failure to timely file a required Form 5472, or filing an incomplete or inaccurate form, results in a statutory penalty of **$25,000 USD per violation per taxable year**, with additional continuing penalties if not cured following IRS notice.

### 2.4. Filing Mechanics (Pro Forma Form 1120)
When a filing obligation arises:
- Complete IRS Form 5472 disclosing the related foreign party (`INDIVIDUAL_OWNER`) and reportable transaction amounts.
- Complete the identification section of **Form 1120 (U.S. Corporation Income Tax Return)**, writing "Foreign-owned U.S. DE" across the top, checking the appropriate foreign-owned disregarded entity boxes, and attaching Form 5472.
- Submit via mail or fax to the specialized IRS international processing center according to current Form 1120 instructions.

## 3. Effectively Connected Income (ECI) & US Trade or Business (ETBUS)

### 3.1. Current Offline / Free Application Posture
- Suda Design currently distributes a free offline application generating $0 revenue globally.
- The company has no physical office, real property, or dependent employees in the United States.
- The LLC is **NOT** currently Engaged in a Trade or Business within the United States (ETBUS) under IRC § 864 and generates **$0 Effectively Connected Income (ECI)**.
- Therefore, no US federal income tax liability is incurred.

### 3.2. Future Monetization Evaluation
- When commercial monetization (such as digital in-app sales or subscriptions) is activated:
  - Income sourcing rules under IRC § 861–865 must be evaluated to determine whether digital sales processed through US payment platforms trigger withholding taxes (e.g., Form 1042 / 1042-S) or require a formal corporate tax return.
  - A comprehensive tax structuring review must be conducted prior to collecting commercial revenue.

## 4. State of Wyoming Tax Framework

Wyoming maintains one of the most advantageous tax environments in the United States:
- **Corporate Income Tax:** **0.00%** (None)
- **Personal Income Tax:** **0.00%** (None)
- **Franchise Tax on Net Income:** **0.00%** (None)
- **Annual Report License Tax:** **Minimum $60.00** per year (W.S. 17-29-209), based on assets located and employed within Wyoming.

## 5. Ongoing Record-Keeping Requirements

Under Treas. Reg. § 1.6038A-3, the company must retain contemporaneous financial records:
1. Complete digital bank statements for all LLC business accounts.
2. Invoices and receipts for all formation fees, registered agent services, and state filing fees.
3. Executed copies of the Operating Agreement and IP Assignment Agreement.
4. Records must be maintained for a statutory minimum of **7 years**.
